Liquid

Liquidity, engineered.

Continuous, two-sided liquidity with tight spreads. Our algorithms adapt to market conditions in real-time, ensuring consistent depth across order books on centralized and decentralized exchanges.

  • 01 Market Making — Continuous two-sided liquidity with tight spreads and real-time adaptation to market conditions.
  • 02 Token Launches — Launch strategies that establish healthy markets and sustainable trading activity from day one.
  • 03 OTC & Treasury — Large block execution with minimal market impact and discreet, efficient settlement.

POL Finance

Quantitative research at the frontier.

Original academic and applied research in liquidity pool dynamics, mean field games, and decentralized systems. We publish our work and build the tools that power our ecosystem.

  • 01 Liquidity Pool Modeling — Probabilistic formulations and replication strategies for constant product markets.
  • 02 Risk Analysis — Quantitative reports on protocol economics, token mechanics, and on-chain risk exposures.
  • 03 Financial Engineering — Designing novel instruments and mechanisms for decentralized capital markets.

Weibull

Insurance reimagined as coordination.

A market-driven protocol for insurance-like contracts. No centralized pricing. No underwriting authority. Transparent infrastructure connecting risk demand with capital provision.

  • 01 Neutral Infrastructure — The protocol doesn't act as insurer or advisor. It provides infrastructure for participants to express risk and execute contracts.
  • 02 Market-Driven Pricing — Capital providers compete through terms they define, while buyers select offers that match their needs.
  • 03 Modular Architecture — Five independent modules handle the full lifecycle from request to settlement.

Loom

Real-world assets, on-chain rails.

Infrastructure for originating and managing receivable purchase agreements. Bridging traditional finance with on-chain settlement, coordination, and risk transfer through the Weibull protocol.

  • 01 RPA Origination — Structured receivable purchase agreements with transparent terms and automated lifecycle management.
  • 02 On-Chain Settlement — Leveraging blockchain infrastructure for efficient, auditable, and programmatic capital flows.
  • 03 Integrated Risk Layer — Native integration with Weibull for insured receivable agreements and decentralized risk coverage.

POL Capital

Capital markets access in Argentina.

Brokerage services and securities trading for institutional and retail investors, with direct access to Argentine capital markets.

  • 01 Brokerage — Equities, fixed income, and derivatives trading with direct BYMA access.
  • 02 Advisory — Investment advisory and portfolio structuring for clients seeking exposure to Argentine and regional markets.
